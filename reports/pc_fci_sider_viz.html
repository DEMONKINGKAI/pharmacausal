What FCI adds: suspected latent confounding among non drug-event pairs

Because drug/event nodes were tier-locked to always orient drug → event, FCI's uncertainty marks show up on other edges instead. ○–○ / ○→ means FCI can't rule out an unmeasured common cause; ↔ means both ends point away from the nodes — a signature of a shared latent cause, not a direct causal link.
